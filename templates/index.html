Facility results will appear here.

To search support facilities along a planned journey, calculate a route and choose Find facilities along this route. Results are mapped records near sampled points along the route, not a guarantee of operational services.

Data note: Results may contain duplicates or incomplete records. Distances are straight-line estimates, not road travel distances. No result does not prove a facility is absent.

Limitations and responsible use

Understand what this academic prototype can and cannot do.

  • This is an academic prototype, not a certified emergency response service.
  • The SOS button records a demonstration event; it does not notify police, contacts, or emergency services.
  • Mapped facilities are not guaranteed to be open, operational, or unique.
  • Geofence alerts are simulated in this browser. Do not rely on this app for personal safety decisions.
